Before you go

Logistics

Sort the e-visas, permits and bike quotes in spring 2027. Everything else can be decided on the road.

Visas

  • Each of you needs an e-visa. Apply only at evisa.gov.vn: $25, single entry, about 3 working days.
  • Entry: Hanoi (Nội Bài). Exit: Đà Nẵng. Print the approval letters.

Licenses

  • Vietnam recognizes only 1968-convention permits. US permits are 1949-convention, so technically not valid.
  • Get AAA permits anyway: $20, two signed passport photos, your US license. Tick Motorcycle and Passenger Car.
  • Buy travel insurance that explicitly covers motorcycle riding. Read the license exclusions.

Bikes

  • Tigit Motorbikes: Hanoi pickup, drop-off at its Đà Nẵng office. Breakdown support and damage waivers.
  • Style Motorbikes: shops in Hanoi, Huế and Hội An. Ask about a Đà Nẵng drop-off and check the specific bike.
  • Ask each shop: total one-way fee, waiver terms without a 1968 permit, breakdown help on remote roads, tires, racks.

Sleeping

  • Every town has nhà nghỉ. Roll up and ask; they register your passports with the police.
  • Summer is Vietnamese holiday season. Arrive by about 5 pm; Booking or Agoda work for same-day backup.
  • Camping is optional, not planned. If you want it, ask homestays in Pu Luong or farmstays near Phong Nha to pitch on their land. No wild camping inside the national park.

Intercoms

  • Packtalk Edge and Packtalk Neo connect over Cardo's mesh: longer range than Bluetooth, and they reconnect on their own after you lose each other.
  • EX-Zero: stick the button mic inside the chin bar with a windsock. Pair and test at home.

Safety

  • Unexploded ordnance still turns up in Quảng Bình and Quảng Trị. Stay on used ground.
  • Riding without a 1968 permit risks a 7-day impound. Carry every document anyway.
  • Don't ride mountain roads after dark. Wait out downpours at a roadside café.
