Arrive · Diocletian's Palace · Board Tango
Morning / Afternoon — Split
We have a few hours before boarding.
Diocletian's Palace — a UNESCO-listed 4th-century Roman complex. Bars, restaurants, and shops woven through living ancient ruins.
Peristyle courtyard — the ceremonial heart of the palace, now an open-air cafe.
Cathedral of Saint Domnius — one of the oldest cathedrals in the world, built inside the emperor's Roman mausoleum.
Walk the Riva waterfront promenade before heading to the boat.
Afternoon — Board Tango & Sail
Meet Goran, get settled, and set sail.
Arrive Hvar for Dinner
Board after 16:00 — plan to land in Split mid-morning, explore the palace, and be at the marina by 4pm.

Hvar → Korčula
Wednesday, August 19
Recovery Morning · Medieval Old Town · Wine Evening
Morning — Hvar
Sleep in. Coffee on deck
Optional: the quieter back streets of Hvar town in the morning, before the tourist crowds arrive.
Afternoon — Korčula
Arrive in the afternoon. Korčula is one of the most beautiful medieval towns on the Adriatic — often called "Little Dubrovnik."
St. Mark's Cathedral — Gothic-Venetian architecture with a stunning rose window. Works attributed to Tintoretto inside.
Marco Polo Tower — Korčula's claim as Marco Polo's birthplace. Great views over the old town rooftops.
Town walls and towers — the Land Gate and Revelin Tower are particularly impressive.
Evening — Korčula
Korčula is home to Pošip and Grk — two indigenous white wines. Wine tasting in the old town.
Dinner at a waterfront konoba.
Nightlife is relaxed — drinks at bars along the old town walls with views over the illuminated sea.

Day
5
Dubrovnik
Friday, August 21
Full Day · Buža Bar · Club Revelin
Morning — Old Town Deep Dive Option
Rector's Palace — finest Gothic-Renaissance building in Dalmatia, 15th century. Seat of the Ragusan Republic. Now a museum.
Dominican Monastery & Museum — stunning 14th-century Gothic cloister. Paintings including a Titian.
Sponza Palace — one of the few buildings to survive the 1667 earthquake. Beautiful 16th-century arcaded facade.
Walk the Stradun — the main marble promenade, polished smooth by centuries of foot traffic.
Afternoon — Breathe
Take the cable car up Mount Srđ for panoramic views over the entire coastline and Old Town.
Swim at Banje Beach just outside Ploče Gate, or kayak around the city walls on the water.
Come back to boat for lunch and to get ready
Evening — The Farewell Night
Buža Bar (7–9pm) — literally a hole cut in the city wall above the sea. Chairs perched on cliff steps over the Adriatic. One of the most singular bar experiences in the world. Go for sunset.
Dinner — Restaurant 360 has tables on the city wall ramparts over the sea. Or Proto on the Stradun for classic Dalmatian seafood.
Club Revelin (midnight+) — set inside a 16th-century fortress at Ploče Gate. International DJs, incredible stone-vaulted interior. Will get a table
